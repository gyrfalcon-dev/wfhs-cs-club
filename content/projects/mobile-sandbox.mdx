Mobile Sandbox is our testing ground for apps that need to feel good in your hand. We use it to try out UI ideas, small utilities, and the kind of features that only make sense after you&apos;ve tapped through them twice.

- Every prototype gets a short writeup so new members can catch up fast.
- Firebase Remote Config helps us flip features on and off without rebuilding the whole app.
