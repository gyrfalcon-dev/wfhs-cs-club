Game Lab is where we make small games feel finished. We keep the scope tight, the feedback honest, and the builds short enough that nobody loses the plot halfway through.

- Sessions usually bounce between art, code, and quick playtests.
- Friday demos help us ship something playable before the week gets away from us.
